Brady's Games

Three separate games. Open one, then add it to your Home Screen.

Brady Goes to Burning ManA 3D open-world adventure: Brady goes to Burning Man for the first time, becomes a burner, and rescues Chinka from the Man. Super Brady-O WorldSuper Brady-O travels the world: a 2D platformer across twelve countries. Art CartBattle-race wild art cars across the playa: every car has its own weapon.

On iPhone: open a game in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Each game installs as its own app.
