# Super Art Kart (formerly Art Cart): Career, Battle Royale, secret levels (design, Nov 11 2026)

Brady's brief, turned into rules. Where he left a choice open, the default chosen here is marked **(default)**; change it if he says otherwise.

## 1. Game structure
Home menu: **CAREER**, **BATTLE ROYALE**, **SECRET LEVELS** (locked until earned), **QUICK RACE** (the existing two cups and Time Trial, kept as they are **(default)**), **POWER-UPS**.

You pick a car and a driver, then play that car's Career and Battle Royale. Progress is per car.

| Part | Stages | Rule to advance | Prize |
|---|---|---|---|
| Career (racing) | Your car's own level first, then the other 11 cars' levels in a random order (fixed when the run starts, saved), then the final **Burn the Man** | Finish a race in the top 3 **(default)**; retry freely | 1 stat point for that car |
| Battle Royale (combat) | Same order rule, in the 12 royale arenas, then the final **The Man** arena | Finish in the top 3 survivors **(default)**; the final must be won outright, then the Man must be destroyed | 1 stat point for that car, and that car's secret level unlocks |
| Secret level | One per car, exactly 3 mini-games: one unique to the level, Vibe Check, Glow Grab | Win each of the 3 | Car is "complete" |

King of the Hill and Capture the Flag are removed from the game. When Career, Battle Royale and the secret level are all won with a car, the game is won with that car (celebration screen), and the player is invited to start with another car.

**Stat points.** Each point is +1 pip on one of that car's four stats (speed, accel, handling, weight), chosen by the player, capped at the 5-pip maximum, refundable/re-assignable in the garage **(default)**. Points apply in every mode. A fully maxed stat row must still be beatable by AI: scale AI a little with the player's total points.

## 2. Levels: one racing level and one royale arena per car
Every level is unique: its own layout, look, obstacles and "enemies" (hazards that move and act; not karts). On a car's level, **that car's signature special is added to everyone's gift-box pool**, so all racers can roll it there.

**Burn the Man (career final).** A dynamite plunger appears at a random spot on the track. Driving into it pushes it; a new one appears elsewhere. Rivals go for it too. The player must push 3 before any rival does **(default: first to 3)**. On the third the Man ignites and burns with fireworks; that is the career win.

**The Man (royale final).** Win the royale, then fight the Man: he has a health bar, stomps, throws fire and embers, and can be damaged by every weapon and item. Destroying him is the Battle Royale win.

## 3. Battle Royale rules
- Combat only: 8 cars, each with a **health bar**; weapons, items and hazards do damage; at zero the car is out. Last cars standing decide the places.
- It must not be a pile-up: arenas at least twice the size of the old battle arenas, spread spawn points, 3 s of spawn protection, repair pickups, AI spread their targets (no more than 2 AI on the player at once), weapons have real range so fights happen across the arena.
- Cars heavier in the weight stat take a little less ram damage; rams do damage in proportion to speed.

The other 14 (and the Man, who is now the boss) stop being selectable and stay in the world as crowd and roadside characters. A saved driver that is no longer selectable falls back to Brady.

Each driver brings **one funny special power-up** (rolls from gift boxes, like the car special) and **one fixed stat point** added to whatever car they drive, so car + driver can be mixed and matched.

## 6. Technical rules
- Original content only; no brands; never copy another game's items, characters or levels. PG-13: "kill" means cartoon knock-outs (lights out, car poofs away), no gore.
- Budgets (iPhone): every view ≤ 420k triangles and ≤ 120 draw calls; no per-frame allocations; procedural geometry through the decor/instancer kit (`src/43_cart_ca_decor.js`); keep gameplay code in new `src/43_cart_*` files that register themselves.
- Old saves must keep loading (trophies and best times stay). New progress lives under `Save.data.cart`.
- Every new mode, level and special gets an automated test (`tools/cart_*_test.js`) that plays it through the debug API and fails on errors, and screenshots that a person looks at.
- Engine files (`src/40_*`, `41_*`, `42_*`) are not ours to edit; requests go in `docs/handoff/REQUESTS.md`.
