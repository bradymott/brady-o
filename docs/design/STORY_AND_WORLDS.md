# Brady-O Burns: world specs for Worlds 2–10

The user's story, expanded into worlds. Missions and names are suggestions: improve them, but keep the story beats.
**Tone:** funny, affectionate satire of Burning Man; short punchy dialogue; never mean-spirited toward real individuals.
**Originality:** everything is our own design. Real camps and art cars are referenced by name only where noted, rendered as our own original designs and never as replicas. No characters, levels or music copied from any other game.

**Story so far (World 1):**
- The Man (the giant wooden effigy) fell in love with Chinka and stole her.
- Brady had no ticket. He found one, beat the greeters and the Man at the gate, and got in.
- The Man fled with Chinka.

**Every world:**
- 5–7 Burn Tokens.
- 3 hidden Photos of Chinka.
- Glow sticks.
- Ground-score baggies.
- Friendly gifting NPCs.
- At least one memorable set piece.

## World 2: FINDING CAMP (the city at night)
**Setting:** Black Rock City at night. Clock streets (2:00–10:00) and lettered streets (Esplanade, A, B, C…), a dense grid of camps, domes, shade structures, RVs, LED-wrapped bikes, burn barrels, fire art, art cars rolling down the streets.
**Goal:** Brady has to find his own camp. All he has is a crumpled note with the address, e.g. "7:30 & E". When he arrives, the Man is waiting there holding Chinka.

**NPCs and enemies:**
- Fire spinners: poi and staff circles as moving hazards.
- Fire dancers.
- Flame-effect installations: timed flame jets.
- Lost burners asking where 4:30 is.
- Rangers: friendly, give directions.
- Bike swarms: traffic hazard; get hit and you're knocked down.
- Drunk camp neighbors.
- "Bacon camp" style gifting NPCs.

**Mission ideas:**
- Ask a Ranger: the street-sign system is introduced.
- The Fire Conclave crossing: cross a big circle of fire spinners.
- Flame Alley: a timed run through an alley of fire cannons.
- Bike Light Roundup: collect 8 lost LED bike lights between bike swarms.
- Find your camp from street-sign clues.
- **Boss:** the Man at camp. Stronger than World 1, with fire and swipe attacks. He escapes again with Chinka.

**Set piece:** an art car parade crossing the street you have to get past.

## World 3: COSTUME CULT & THE YELLOW BIKE (day)
**Setting:** daytime city.
- The Costume Cult camp: racks of costumes, mirrors, fog machines, a catwalk.
- A bike repair camp.
- The open playa with yellow community bikes scattered and in use.

**Goal:**
1. Brady has to blend in so the Man won't spot him. He picks a costume (the engine's costume chooser) and keeps it on for the rest of the game.
2. Then he has to find a yellow bike, which he can ride for the rest of the game.

**Mission ideas:**
- Dress the Part: costume chooser plus a catwalk strut moment.
- Vibe Check: in costume, sneak past the Man's lookouts (stealth with vision cones).
- Yellow Bike Hunt: follow clues to a yellow bike.
- The Bike Thief: chase a guy who rides off on it.
- Fix a Flat: find a patch kit at the bike repair camp.
- First Ride: a timed bike course.

**Enemies:**
- Fashion police, comedic: they judge outfits.
- Bike thieves.
- The Man's lookouts.

## World 4: THE ORGY DOME (night)
Keep it comedic and tasteful. No nudity, no explicit content; innuendo and silliness only.

**Setting:**
- A district of domes and lounges around a big pink dome with a neon sign.
- Velvet ropes, chill lounges, a cuddle puddle in fog and red light.

**Goal:** meet an informant who knows where the Man is keeping Chinka. Avoid the creepy dudes: guys in fur coats and nothing much else, "free hugs" signs, "hey, wanna come to a cuddle puddle?".

**Mechanics:**
- Stealth: creeps have vision cones. If one spots you, he follows and blathers at you, draining hydration.
- Friendly consent-checkers at the door explain the rules (a funny, positive bit).
- Collect 8 lost goggles for the lost & found.

**Boss:** "The Velvet Creeper", a giant sleazy guy in a huge fur coat with a pack of creepers. He pulls you in with a lasso of fluffy boa and throws bad pickup lines as projectiles.

**Reward:** the informant gives a clue toward Costco Soul Trading Co.

## World 5: COSTCO SOUL TRADING CO. (day)
**Setting:**
- A giant gifting "warehouse" camp (parody): towering shelves of donated stuff, membership-card greeters, free-sample stations, bulk aisles, forklift pallets.
- Its own design; the name is a nod to the real camp.

**Goal:** meet a platonic temporary buddy, an original friendly burner (give them a fun name and a look). They become a companion who follows Brady and helps him find something: the location of the giant fire-breathing octopus art car.

**Mission ideas:**
- Membership Card: get through the greeter.
- Sample Line Sprint: a timed tasting run.
- Stack the Pallets: platforming up shelves.
- Find the buddy's lost something.
- Escort the buddy through the bulk aisle (rolling carts are hazards).
- The buddy shows Brady a high lookout. Climb it to spot the octopus's fire on the horizon.

**Enemies:**
- Cart-pushing hoarders.
- "Gifting is not bartering" police.
- Shoplifters stealing gifts.

## World 6: THE ART CAR HUNT (night, deep playa)
**Setting:** the deep playa at night: sculptures, art cars, lasers, dust.
**Goal:** find and commandeer a giant fire-breathing mechanical octopus art car. It is an original design: a big metal octopus on wheels, tentacles waving, fire bursting from its head. Players call it "El Pulpo" as a nickname. Tech bros on electric unicycles keep trying to pitch you their blockchain and AI startups; their pitch bubbles are projectiles that stun ("It's like Uber for dust").

**Mission ideas:**
- Follow the Fire: track the octopus by its flame bursts.
- Tech Bro Gauntlet.
- Climb aboard while it's moving (up the tentacles).
- Convince the driver: bring him water.
- Drive the octopus: vehicle section using the engine's art car.
- Fire Show: use its flame cannon to light 6 burn barrels.

**Boss:** "Chad, Founder & CEO": a mega tech bro on a giant electric unicycle with VC drones. Pitch-deck projectiles.

## World 7: SUNRISE SET (dawn)
**Setting:** dawn on the deep playa. A legendary sunrise set on a heart-topped sound art car (our own design). Crowds dancing, sunrise colors, dust in golden light.
**About the DJ:** respectful name-drop only. NPC chatter like "Lee Burridge is playing the sunrise set on the heart!" is fine. The DJ is a small silhouette at the decks: no caricature, no dialogue attributed to him.

**Enemies:**
- Sparkle ponies: beg for water, steal items.
- Influencers taking photos: camera-flash stun, ring-light glare, they block paths for selfies.
- Crusty old burners: "It was better in '98!" rants that slow you.

**Mission ideas:**
- Make it to the heart car before sunrise: a timed ride on the bike.
- Water for the sparkle ponies: collect 8 bottles.
- Photobomb Dodge: an influencer gauntlet.
- Out-argue the crusty burners: dialogue-choice comedy.
- Dance-off: a short rhythm minigame (tap on the beat).
- Climb to the top deck of the heart car as the sun rises.

**Boss:** "The Glitter Queen", an influencer with a drone swarm and a ring-light beam.

## World 8: HELL STATION (hot day)
**Setting:** the BRC fuel depot and the DPW yard. Fuel tanks, pumps, heavy machinery, forklifts, containers, rebar, trailers, dust.
**Goal:** fuel up the commandeered octopus art car.
**DPW:**
- Exaggerated for comedy: very rude, very dusty, haven't showered since July, live in their trailers out here year-round, and really, really mean.
- They hate Brady on sight. Each takes multiple hits and has a heavy attack.
- They throw wrenches, swing rebar, and drive forklifts at you.

**Mission ideas:**
- Get in the fuel line.
- Get the fuel permit: a fetch chain with DPW paperwork.
- Forklift platforming.
- Defend the art car while it fuels (wave defense).
- Steal back your fuel nozzle.

**Boss:** "DPW Foreman Rusty" on a telehandler, plus his crew.

## World 9: DEEP PLAYA SHOWDOWN (night, dust storm)
**Setting:** a night dust storm on the open playa. Lamp posts, the trash fence at the edge, sculptures looming out of the dust. The Temple glows far away. It's a quiet, sacred space: no fighting at or in the Temple; Brady may pass by it respectfully.
**Goal:** the Man appears again with Chinka and drags her toward his pavilion. Fight him in the storm. He escapes to his pavilion for the finale.

**Mission ideas:**
- Follow the lamp line in a whiteout.
- Rescue 5 burners lost in the dust.
- Reach the trash fence.
- Find shelter from a dust devil.

**Boss:** the Man in the storm. He vanishes into the dust and reappears, and dust devils are among his attacks.

## World 10: THE MAN PAVILION (finale, night of the Burn)
**Setting:** the center of the city on Burn night. Crowds in a huge circle, fire dancers, art cars ringing the center, fireworks.
**Goal:** Brady rides his yellow bike in his costume across the playa to the Man pavilion.

**Final battle:**
- **Phase 1:** the Man stands on top of a flying-saucer-like base: our own design, inspired by the idea of an alien landing, not a replica of any real base. He attacks from up there.
- **Phase 2:** the Man grows into a giant.
  - Neon flashes that destroy Brady unless he takes cover behind objects.
  - Fire blasts from his face.
  - Fireworks from his arms.
  - Very hard but fair; Brady wins because he's amazing.

**Ending:** the Man is defeated, Chinka is rescued, and the Man BURNS. Fireworks, crowd cheers, Brady and Chinka watch together. Then the ending and credits.

### W1, The Line (engine author's world)
Brady and Chinka rode in on an RV belonging to the RED LIGHT DISTRICT camp: a burlesque/cabaret camp of glamorous blonde performers in pink bikinis, feather boas and red lingerie-style corsets. They're friendly, funny and protective of Brady. Keep it PG-13: glam, not explicit.

### W2, No Camp (worlds 2–4 agent; replaces "Finding Camp")
1. **The stash.** Brady is a first-timer with no camp. He stashes his gear in the Red Light District RV; the crew babysits his stuff ("Honey, you have NO camp?").
2. **The search.** He wanders the city at night looking for a camp to take him: rejections ("we're full", "did you pay dues?", "it's a 10-year waitlist"), fire spinners, lamplighters, darkwads.
3. **Center Camp.** He gives up and sleeps on a couch at Center Camp. Comedic: wakes up to slam poetry.
4. **The tent guy.** A guy who "feels bad for him" offers space in his tent. Brady treks back to the RV to haul his stuff over (an encumbered carry mission across the city).
5. **The escape.** In the night Brady wakes up to the guy sniffing his feet. Comedic and non-sexual: he claims he's "reading your aura through your soles". Brady flees into the night with his stuff (a chase).
6. **The lead.** It ends with Brady hearing distant bamboo-floor bass, which leads to Rhythm Wave.

### W16, Rhythm Wave (new agent)
- **The camp:** a camp with a gorgeous BAMBOO DANCE FLOOR. The leader WOLFIE lets Brady stay if he:
  - helps build the camp: construction platforming, carrying bamboo, raising the shade;
  - wins the "Yoga Yoni Tantra Enlightenment Dance Contest", a dance/rhythm minigame where Brady must dance well to impress the campers.
- **The pill:** after he wins, BAM BAM, an original burner in a wild caveman costume (fur tunic, bone necklace, giant foam club; NOT the cartoon character's look), offers him a pill of "sassafras": "Take HALF." Brady takes the whole thing (off-screen gulp, spiral eyes).
- **The ride:** the world goes "wonky as a donkey". Brady must find a yellow bike and ride it, wobbly and trippy, across the playa to the GIANT GLOWING BOUNCY MUSHROOMS in the distance (the big-art Glowshroom Grove).
- **Ground scores:** this is where ground scores (baggies) start appearing in the game. Brady can stash them in his BACKPACK, trade them to other burners for different stuff, or take them. Some challenges require taking one.
