## A. Hidden lands

### A1. Wrong Side of the Wash

**Pitch:** Piwi hung the tour laundry across two rooftops. An entire neighbourhood lives on the side nobody sees.  
**Look:** Vermilion pegs, cream linen rectangles, navy checks and a mint sky. Foes behind a sheet leave a single broad silhouette.

**Levels:**

1. **Peg Legwork.** Push into a checkered drying net to cling, steer across it, and press JUMP in a torn square to pass through to the other face. Late on, a peg beetle blocks both routes with its trousers until you squeeze its clip from behind with THROW.
2. **Cold Wash Only.** Brady’s weight stretches a wet sweater in the direction he walks, so stretch one sleeve to the next roof and jump off its cuff. The last sweater has a short sleeve on one side and only stretches far enough if you start at the opposite shoulder.
3. **Loose End.** Hold THROW on a loose thread and walk to unzip a seam into a long strip of floor. The last hem has two threads, and pulling the one under Brady’s feet opens a short drop to a lower retry ledge.
4. **The Cling Section.** Run on wool to charge Brady’s socks, then leap close to a felt patch to stick where he lands. The final wall has broad patches separated by bare washing labels, so drop to the wool sill for a fresh charge before the last leap.
5. **Pocket Address.** Jump into a hanging pocket to pop out of the pocket with the matching lining, visible whenever its lip flops open. Two final pockets share a stripe, but only one has the matching scalloped hem as well.
6. **Fitted Sheet for One.** Tuck a boss corner over a bedpost by luring it there and jumping through its elastic loop. The fourth corner starts lifting the first unless Brady stands inside the last loose pocket.

**Boss:** Her Fitted Majesty. **1** — One corner draws taut into an arrow before it snaps straight ahead. Sidestep at a post to snag it. **2** — Two corners pucker into rings before a double sweep. Duck through the wide first loop and jump through the second to catch both posts. **3** — The remaining pocket balloons around a visible bedpost before she shakes the bed. Step inside the pocket and jump on the shake to tuck the last corner. She lies perfectly flat and immediately complains of a wrinkle.  
**Critters:** Peg Beetle wears trousers three sizes too big. Lint Lamb grows a fleece by walking and sneezes it all off. Hanger Heron straightens a crooked hanger even while chasing Brady. Sock Moth nibbles one peephole, then insists on looking through it.  
**Hidden entrance:** In France, climb the bakery’s rear balcony and jump upward through the low hem of a hanging sheet. One peg casts a shadow on the *front* of the fabric.

### A2. Postmark Sound

**Look:** Butter-yellow paper, cobalt wave scallops, coral stamp teeth and black postmark rings.

**Levels:**

2. **Stamp Duty.** Walk a loose stamp into position on the left page, then press THROW to print its raised shape as a solid foothold on the facing page. The last foothold is L-shaped and prints in reverse, so preview its ink silhouette before pressing.
3. **Blotter Bay.** Walk the blotting pad beneath an advancing bead of ink to drink a gap in the printed surf and let a paper boat through. The last wave arrives in two beads and the pad dries only when you jump and slap it against the overhead blotter.
4. **Forwarding Fold.** At a crease, hold THROW and walk to fold the far bank toward Brady, then step across the meeting edges. The last fold tucks the exit inside the card unless you stand on its printed side before releasing.
5. **Mismeasured Coast.** Walk either way on a map scale to enlarge or shrink the printed harbour around a fixed brass pin, then step off to lock the scale. At the last pin, enlarge a gap enough to pass underneath before shrinking the far quay to jumping distance.
6. **Return to Sender.** Make an incoming postmark land across both halves of a torn address so the postcard sends Piwi’s stamp pad home. On the last impression the halves are on different folds until Brady stands on their common crease.

**Boss:** The Postmaster’s Thumb. **1** — A dotted ring settles before the thumb stamps straight down. Lure it onto the first torn address and step out. **2** — An inked edge tilts before it rolls a stripe across the desk. Jump the stripe from the crease that brings address two together. **3** — Two pale half-rings appear before a double impression. Hold the common crease for the first mark, then step clear of the second. The completed address mails the thumb back to the other end of its own arm.  
**Critters:** Gum Gull sticks its beak shut after every peck. Margin Crab refuses to step over a ruled line. Blot Pup leaves a pawprint and goes back to sniff it. Crease Eel can only turn at a fold.  
**Hidden entrance:** In Vietnam, stand on the reverse side of the last waterside postcard rack and walk into its lowest card. One printed wave continues across the card’s white border.

### A3. The Orchard After Closing

**Levels:**

3. **Cut Above.** Pinch a marked bud with THROW and its two side shoots grow outward into steps. The final branch needs its lower bud intact to support Brady while he pinches the top one.

5. **The Graft Takes.** Press THROW where two swaying cut branches meet to bind them into a living arch, then cross it. The last arch must join matching bark notches while Brady’s weight pulls one cut below the other.
6. **Windfall Committee.** Pluck a loaded branch from underneath so its spring flicks the fruit into a voting basket. The final committee member leans away from the basket and must be persuaded to lean back.

**Boss:** Three pears sharing one stalk. **1** — A pear raises its leaf before it drops onto a branch. Get underneath and pluck after it lands to flick it into the basket. **2** — The next pear blushes on one cheek before lunging to that side. Stand basket-side until it commits, then pluck the branch it lands on. **3** — The last pear extends its stalk like a pointing finger before it lunges away from Brady. **Critters:** Pear Fly takes a bite, then flies backwards to admire the hole. Tie Worm tightens one garden tie and gets stuck in its own bow. Pip Finch spits the pip back into the fruit. Bark Snail waits for a branch to move underneath it and claims the distance.  
**Hidden entrance:** In Japan, walk behind the orchard’s last tree and jump into the underside of its lowest fork. One pear hangs from a garden tie instead of a stalk.

### A4. Foley Beach

**Boss:** Mr. Thunder, a pea in a biscuit tin. **1** — His lid lifts before a broadside clap. **Critters:** Clapper Clam snaps a second after everyone has gone home. Hiss Kettle startles itself into silence. Rubbersole Ray squeaks only when it tries to tiptoe. Boom Crab follows the loudest prop with its microphone held backwards.  
**Hidden entrance:** In Brazil, jump through the painted sea behind the beach band’s empty riser. A single wave has a brass fastener in its foam.

## B. Secret-room mini-games

Times are target lengths for a successful play. Show the rule in motion while Brady enters. In a mounted game the stick aims or selects, JUMP hops or confirms, and THROW plays the prop. A miss resets that turn.

### Memory

1. **Cup with a Limp** — The pea starts under the cup that rocks on a chipped foot. Follow that cup through two three-cup shuffles and pick it at the end of each. **Mozambique · 15s**
2. **Room with a View** — A train passes three station windows. When their shutters close, hop onto pictures of the three passengers in arrival order. **Japan · 20s**

## E. Other ideas

1. **Let one discovery change the journey home.** After the orchard, the lone tied pear at its entrance has grown into a little bench. A player passing through Japan again gets to sit on the secret they found.
2. **Give Pupo the travel album.** On the land map, each discovered place contributes one small object to his saddle blanket. By the end he is carrying a whole holiday, including a fork he maintains is a comb.
3. **Give three postcards a second picture.** When a level’s three cards sit edge to edge, their cancelled stamps form a tiny scene across the seams. Finding the last one finishes a visual joke even before the land’s care package arrives.
4. **Give the near and far sides different acting.** On the net, a far-side beetle presses its trousers flat against the sheet before it crosses. A player can anticipate the collision and enjoy the beetle’s predicament in the same glance.
5. **Let a failed guess land somewhere funny.** A wrong pocket in the wash drops Brady onto a soft heap of Piwi’s socks within sight of the correct lining. The miss teaches the difference and the next try is a jump away.
6. **Put a souvenir in the boss’s hands.** On a return visit the repaired umbrella keeps the inspector dry, and the fossil is still enjoying its first comfortable sit. The player gets to see what winning did for somebody.
