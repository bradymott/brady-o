# Super Brady-O World • Hidden land reference sheets

Each numbered folder contains three full-resolution PNGs. Open the image in its own window to see the poses and prop details at full size.

- **cast.png** reads across in five columns. The upper row is idle. Directly below is the same character acting, attacking or reacting. The rightmost column is the boss.
- **building-blocks.png** reads across the top for ground, platform and hazard, then across the bottom for the climbable or rideable object, full prize box and empty prize box.
- **entrance.png** pairs a view in the existing land with a close-up of the clue. The enlarged detail is for the art team. Keep the clue at the scale of the surrounding props in the game.
