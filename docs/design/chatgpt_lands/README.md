# Reference sheets from ChatGPT for the twelve hidden lands

One folder per land (`a1`..`a12`, numbered as in `docs/design/CHATGPT_RELAY.md` section A). Each holds up to three sheets: `entrance-clue` (the one prop that marks the way in, in its existing land), `cast` (four critters and the boss: top row idle, bottom row attacking or defeated) and `blocks` (ground, platform, hazard, climbable, prize box closed and empty). They are REFERENCE: redraw in the game's code-drawn style; never embed them. Brady, Chinka-O, Pupo and Piwi keep the game's own models.

- `a12-spare-bone-quarry/`: clue = a paw print pressed into an ochre rock above a cave mouth on a highland path (snow-capped volcano behind: Ecuador). Cast = a mouse living in a hollow bone (springs out on a tail), a spotted gecko whose tail drops off in segments, a grumpy orange-handled dig brush that sweeps dust, a dark green bird with an orange eye-ring that pecks at a specimen dish, and the boss: a mis-assembled fossil skeleton with persimmon joint caps and a museum label on its head, who ends up sitting down. Blocks = ochre strata ground with a green ammonite, a bone-shelf platform on a ball joint, a snapping jawbone hazard on an orange hinge, a rib-and-joint ladder, a dark green chest with a bone latch (closed / open with the bone hanging).
- `a11-table-for-two/`: clue = on a sunny Spanish terrace (striped awning, bougainvillea, a hilltop fortress far off), one café table is laid for one, but its shadow on the paving shows TWO hands clasped. Cast = a crouton waiter in a navy waistcoat and red bow tie who sweeps crumbs under a cloche, a peppercorn bug on spring legs with a red nose who sneezes pepper, a gravy-boat dachshund (cream china, navy patches) who rolls over, a butter-curl snail with long lashes who melts into a puddle, and the boss: a tall navy-handled fork with a face who bows right over when offered the last olive. Blocks = a table with a red gingham cloth (ground), a chipped blue-rimmed plate (platform), a navy fork stabbing down onto red warning marks (hazard), a bread sponge soaking in oil (bouncy/sinking), a navy butter dish with a cream lid and an olive knob on a red-striped foot (prize box: closed / lid tipped off).
- `a10-department-of-unclaimed-shadows/` (pictures seen in chat only, not on disk): clue = on the grey Moon plain a lone steel locker casts a long black shadow, and the shadow has a small white KEYHOLE in it (the locker itself has none). Cast (grape-purple silhouettes, salmon and butter-yellow accents) = a shadow rabbit whose ears turn into a second head (a hand-shadow), a salmon stapler dachshund that clamps down on paper, a yellow lamp-bug that casts a scuttling shadow-bug below it, a grumpy purple crow with paper-folder wings that carries off a key, and the boss: a shadow gentleman in a straw boater and salmon tie who splits into two (one yellow hat, one salmon). Blocks = a salmon filing cabinet with a yellow top and a notch (ground), a torn purple shadow scrap (platform), a salmon stapler jaw with purple teeth (hazard), drawer handles joined by a stepped purple shadow (climbable), a salmon drawer with a yellow front showing a boot silhouette (prize box: closed / pulled open with the silhouette slipping out).
- `a9-table-salt-sea/` (pictures seen in chat only): clue = at a Moroccan market stall of oranges, a small clay bowl of salt holds ONE clear crystal with a tiny sea horizon inside it. (The concept shows a tower far off through an arch: in the game keep any such building a plain distant silhouette, or leave it out.) Cast = a blue bird perched on a salt cube that pecks it to crumbs, a pink shrimp that curls up in a spoon, a cork-shelled beetle with a corkscrew nose that pops out of a cork, a salt-cube creature with a long tongue that licks crystals, and the boss: a cork chef with a moustache, toque and apron who sprinkles "just a pinch". Blocks = white salt crystal ground with a blue water seam, salt-cube stair steps (platform), a dark shaker lid pouring a stream of crystals (hazard), a floating cork raft in blue water, a blue glass jar with a cork lid and dark handle full of crystals (prize box: closed / lid off and empty).
