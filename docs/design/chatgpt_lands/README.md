# Reference sheets from ChatGPT for the twelve hidden lands

One folder per land (`a1`..`a12`, numbered as in `docs/design/CHATGPT_RELAY.md` section A). Each holds up to three sheets: `entrance-clue` (the one prop that marks the way in, in its existing land), `cast` (four critters and the boss: top row idle, bottom row attacking or defeated) and `blocks` (ground, platform, hazard, climbable, prize box closed and empty). They are REFERENCE: redraw in the game's code-drawn style; never embed them. Brady, Chinka-O, Pupo and Piwi keep the game's own models.

- `a12-spare-bone-quarry/`: clue = a paw print pressed into an ochre rock above a cave mouth on a highland path (snow-capped volcano behind: Ecuador). Cast = a mouse living in a hollow bone (springs out on a tail), a spotted gecko whose tail drops off in segments, a grumpy orange-handled dig brush that sweeps dust, a dark green bird with an orange eye-ring that pecks at a specimen dish, and the boss: a mis-assembled fossil skeleton with persimmon joint caps and a museum label on its head, who ends up sitting down. Blocks = ochre strata ground with a green ammonite, a bone-shelf platform on a ball joint, a snapping jawbone hazard on an orange hinge, a rib-and-joint ladder, a dark green chest with a bone latch (closed / open with the bone hanging).
