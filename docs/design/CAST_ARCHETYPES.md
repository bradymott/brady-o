# Brady-O Burns: burner archetypes from the user

The user wrote these six profiles: "funny profiles for burners Brady-O will encounter along his journey".
- Use them as NPCs, enemies, quest-givers and running gags. Each world builder adds the ones assigned below, in their worlds.
- Each gets a distinct rounded 3D look on the shared rig, plus short, funny dialogue in our own words, built from the profile details.
- Keep the humor affectionate satire.
- No real brand names in-game (no "Prada", no "Starlink"): say "designer goggles" and "satellite internet dish".

## 1. The Plug-and-Play "Turnkey" Billionaire
- **Look:** pristine white linens, designer aviators, a silk dust mask, designer goggles, and a custom e-bike that costs more than a used car.
- **Camp:** a fenced-off, air-conditioned geodesic dome compound, with security guards at the gate and a "radically inclusive" open bar that they won't let you into.
- **Reality:** flew in on a private jet. Hired "sherpas" to build the camp, cook, and empty the RV septic. Talks passionately about radical inclusion while security turns people away.
- **Gameplay ideas:**
  - Sneak or talk your way into the compound.
  - Get past the guards with a disguise or a gift.
  - The tech bro is always pitching him.
  - A sherpa NPC who secretly helps you.

## 2. The Crusty "Back In My Day" Vet
- **Look:** a faded utility kilt, a heavy canvas utility belt from 1997, and permanent playa dust ground into the skin.
- **Camp:** a structurally terrifying tower of rusted scaffolding and recycled pallets. Make it a climbable landmark that wobbles.
- **Reality:**
  - Hasn't read the event guide since 2004.
  - Drinks warm beer.
  - Yells at art cars for being "too corporate".
  - Tells everyone "the burn peaked when it was still on Baker Beach".
- **Gameplay idea:** his rants slow you down. Win him over with a ridiculously old-school gift (a mixtape? a Polaroid?) to get something.

## 3. The Sparkle Pony
- **Look:** high-fashion neon faux fur, body glitter, fishnets, and platform boots that are hopeless on cracked desert ground.
- **Camp:** a giant chaotic pile of tulle and glitter inside a tent they can't zip shut.
- **Reality:** brought three suitcases of costumes but no water, cup or flashlight. Survives on other camps' charity, trading "good vibes" for food, shelter and bike-flat fixes.
- **Gameplay ideas:**
  - They beg for water.
  - They steal power-ups.
  - Helping them can pay off ("good vibes" are a currency).

## 4. The Silicon Valley "Optimization" Tech Bro
- **Look:** a utility vest with 47 pockets, a wearable air purifier, and a smartwatch tracking heart-rate variability during a dust storm.
- **Camp:** a hyper-efficient solar-powered grid with its own private satellite internet dish.
- **Reality:**
  - Sees the burn as "a networking opportunity with zero cellular friction".
  - Calls a drum circle "decentralized community architecture".
  - Spends the week pitching a Web3 startup to the Turnkey Billionaire.
- **Gameplay idea:** pitch-bubble projectiles stun ("It's like Uber, but for dust").

## 5. The "Over-Prepared" Safety Third Captain
- **Look:** reflective tape on every surface, heavy combat boots, an industrial respirator, and a radio strapped to the chest.
- **Camp:** impeccably organized, with strict gray-water evaporation protocols and laminated chore wheels.
- **Reality:** so focused on emergency prep, logistics and MOOP (Matter Out Of Place) sweeps that they forget to have fun. Will aggressively lecture you if your bike light is 5 lumens too dim.
- **Gameplay ideas:**
  - Lectures freeze you in place.
  - Quest-giver for a MOOP sweep (pick up 10 bits of litter) or a "get a brighter bike light" errand.
  - Could be a mini-boss who lectures in waves.

## 6. The "Ego-Death" Spiritual Healer
- **Look:** flowing white robes, a crystal necklace the size of a fist, and bare feet (despite the playa dust).
- **Camp:** a sound-healing dome that smells entirely of sage and essential oils.
- **Reality:**
  - Has achieved total enlightenment and makes sure you know it.
  - Offers unsolicited eye-gazing sessions.
  - Cries at the beauty of a passing trash truck.
  - Hunts for a "twin flame" they'll ghost the Monday after the event.
- **Gameplay idea:** eye-gazing is a "stare" beam that roots you in place until you mash to break eye contact.
