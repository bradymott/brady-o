# Backstage, Wormholes, Brady Buttons and Festival Fields: what is built and where to look

Branch `sbw/backstage` (17 Nov 2026). Built from `docs/design/SECRETS_WARPS_SWITCHES.md` (Brady's decisions at its top win) and the contracts in `docs/design/SECRETS_CONTRACTS.md`. Player-facing names: **Wormholes**, **Brady Buttons**, **Piwi's House Rules**. Code names stay neutral (`warp`, `button`, `seam`, `backstage`).

## Where to look (tell Brady)

Every secret has a tell you can see from the normal route. After a level is cleared once, the map's exit dots (○ / ●) count its Wormhole too.

### Mexico 1-4 Volcán Escape: the Rigging (backstage above the volcano)
- **The way in:** climb to the crater rim (the very top, by the finish gate). In the rim's left corner a **sandbag dangles down on a rope** from above the top of the screen, over a **spring**, next to a yellow **CREW ONLY** sign. Stand on the spring and **hold jump**: Brady flies off the top of the screen and a velvet curtain whips away: he is in the Rigging.
- **Inside:** the catwalks over the volcano set, a ghost light, a costume rail, Piwi's wind-up stagehand mice (harmless; they squeak and run). Climb the catwalks (or bounce off the crash mat in the deck, hold jump) to the high fly gallery.
- **Brady Button (Rule 3: NO DANCING):** on the high fly gallery. Stand beside it and press **Action** (or Up). Jumping on it only goes "bzzt". It shows the REVOKED card, then a little world map where **Festival Fields** cracks open. From then on every critter dances (Settings → HATS & DANCING to switch the effects off).
- **Wormhole → ECUADOR (2-7 Devil's Cauldron):** the trunk at the far right end of the deck; press **Up**. Brady is sucked in, the results say **WORMHOLE!**, then the map shows the wormhole crossing the globe and Brady pops out in Ecuador at 2-7 (a trail from Ecuador's airstrip to 2-7 opens so he is never stranded). 1-4 counts as cleared.
- **Way back:** the rope through the hole in the deck at the left (where he came up): drop through it and he lands back on the crater rim.

### Festival Fields (bonus land 23), opened by the Mexico button
On the world route map, a new island in the North Atlantic. Fly there from any land.

**23-1 Campsite Scramble** (a maze of six fields, any order)
- Piwi padlocked the main stage with three padlocks (red, blue, gold). Their **tent pegs** are lost round the campsite:
  - **red:** guy-rope alley (top middle field), on the roof of the big tent: hold jump on the guy-rope spring under it.
  - **gold:** the chill-out field (bottom left), on the high platform: bounce off the hammocks in the ground (hold jump).
  - **blue:** the airbed pond (bottom right), on the platform over the pond: bounce off the airbeds (hold jump).
  - The tent flap in guy-rope alley goes down to the lost-property yard (bottom middle), which joins the chill-out field and the pond.
- Postcards: P1 up the pallet steps in the gate field; P2 behind the fake wall top right of the lost-property yard; P3 in the wings (below).
- **The Wings (backstage) and a Wormhole → MEXICO (1-4):** at the very start, the curtain at the left edge of the gate field stirs and a stagehand mouse runs that way. Walk **left** off the start. Inside: the stage's wings, a Wormhole back to Mexico 1-4, a hidden 1UP over the ghost light, P3 up the catwalks. Walk off the right edge to go back.

**23-2 The Mud Bath** (a maze of mud pools that rise in every rain squall)
- The boardwalk at the start is broken: drop through the gap into the wallow. Every 16 s the mud rises to the top of the pools, holds 4 s and drains. The gaps high in the pool walls can only be swum through at high mud. Flipper fins on the stand at the start help.
- The tent flap at the bottom of the duck pond (middle) goes up to the showers and on to the exit tent.
- P1 over the shower stalls; P3 under the ceiling of the wellies pool (bottom right; high mud only); a hidden 1UP there too.
- **The Under-Stage (backstage):** the **drain swirling in the bottom of the duck pond**: swim or drop down into it (press down). Brady comes out under the main stage: P2, a hidden 1UP, a stage door back to the pond.

**23-3 The Silent Disco** (night; a dark maze of six fields)
- Piwi switched the music off. Brady's glow fades in the dark; touching a **glow-stick lantern** relights it. The dance floors still light up to a beat nobody can hear: cross when they light.
- Route: the start field → the dance-floor bridge over the spikes (P1 above it) → the tent flap at its end down to the glow-stick grove → right to the speaker stacks → climb the stacks and the dance floor to the tent flap at the top → the DJ booth and the exit gate.
- The chill tent (bottom left, off the grove) is a dead end with P2 behind a fake wall top left and a hidden 1UP; P3 is at the top of the speaker stacks.

**23-4 The Sound Limiter** (the boss; the main stage)
- A short walk past the stalls (three postcards on the way), then Piwi's noise-complaint robot: a giant volume knob on long legs.
- It SHUSHES (hush waves roll both ways along the stage: jump them), HOPS at Brady, and every so often stops to LISTEN: its dial lights up, and that is the moment to stomp the dial. Any other stomp bounces off. Six stomps; at four it adds FEEDBACK (music notes arc out). The last stomp turns it up to eleven and it blows its fuse.
- Beating it completes Festival Fields.

Climb the cases, cross the truss over the gate **without touching the gate**, drop down past it and keep walking right off the edge: the Loading Dock. Its Wormhole (far right) leads to:

### Japan 11-4 Neon Tower: the Rigging above the rooftop (Brady Button, Rule 10)
- **The way in:** climb all the way to the rooftop (the finish gate is up there). On the left of the roof a **red aircraft-warning lamp blinks on its mast** at the top of the screen, and an **air-con unit's steam plume** rises off the top; under them is a **spring** by a yellow **CREW ONLY** sign. Stand on the spring and **hold jump**: Brady flies off the top of the screen into the Rigging over the tower set.
- **Inside:** catwalks over the tower, the warning lamp's mast, air-con units, a ghost light, Piwi's stagehand mice. Climb the catwalks (or bounce off the crash mat in the deck, hold jump) to the high gallery.
- **Brady Button (Rule 10: NO LATE FLIGHTS):** on the high gallery. Stand beside it and press **Action** (or Up). It shows the REVOKED card ("NIGHT FLIGHTS NOW LEAVE FROM EVERY CLEARED LAND!"), then a little world map where **The Red-Eye** pops up over the North Pacific.
- **What else it changes:** on the world route map, **night flights**: thin red dashed routes join every land whose boss Brady has beaten, a flight between two such lands is a quick hop under a darkened sky ("NIGHT FLIGHT TO ..."), and a **little red-eye plane with a blinking red light circles the globe**. (The game already let Brady fly to any land he had opened, so the night flights are quicker, not new routes.)
- **Way back:** the rope through the hole in the deck at the left drops back down onto the rooftop. No Wormhole here: Japan's Wormhole to Nepal is on 11-3's Loading Dock.

### The Red-Eye (bonus land 25), opened by the Japan button
On the world route map, a jet over the North Pacific (fly there from any land). Its map is a cutaway jumbo jet, nose to tail: up the airstairs at the tail, along the cabin, out onto the wing, down to the cargo hold, the flight deck at the nose. Everyone on board is an animal. It is the hardest bonus land.

**25-1 Aisle Seat** (a chase)
- The drinks trolley has broken loose and comes down the aisle after Brady, pushing a heap of suitcases and pillows. It never stops: keep going right. It costs a hit if it catches you and shoves you on ahead.
- On the way: rows of seats to hop (sleeping animals in them: a bear in an eye mask, an owl with a book, a rabbit in headphones, a tortoise in a neck pillow), grumpy geese in neck pillows who HONK and charge, overhead bins that rattle and drop a suitcase when you pass under them, a sticky cola spill across the aisle (jump it from the seat tops), a sleepy sloth inching along the bin rail (ride it up).
- P1: on the high bin above the **snoring walrus** (his belly is a trampoline: land on him and hold jump). P3: on the high bin you reach from the **sloth**.
- **Secret:** halfway, by the galley, a navy **curtain** stirs next to a CREW ONLY sign: press Up there. Behind it, the **crew rest**: bunks up the wall, everyone asleep. P2 on the top bunk, a hidden 1UP, a star in a block. The curtain takes you back out (the trolley waits while you're in there).

**25-2 Turbulence** (work out the route)
- The jet's three decks in one cutaway: the main cabin (the start, at the back), the upper deck in the hump at the front, the cargo hold underneath. The exit is the **flight deck door at the front of the upper deck**.
- Every few seconds the **FASTEN SEAT BELT** sign (top of the screen) blinks and dings, then the cabin **lurches**: everyone is shoved sideways (hold **DOWN** to brace and you don't move), and three loose things slide to their other spot: the **galley cart** that bridges a hole in the cabin floor, the **jammed cart** blocking the passage upstairs, and a **cargo container** in the hold. Stand on a sliding cart and you ride with it; if one slides into you, you end up on top of it.
- **Two ways to the front:** (1) up the **spiral stairs** in the middle of the cabin to the upper-deck lounge, then slip past the **jammed cart** in two goes (it slides back and forth with each lurch); or (2) down into the **cargo hold** (through the hole in the cabin floor, or the **galley lift**), where the container must slide next to the **crate stack** so you can climb over it, then the **cargo ladder** up into first class and the **front stairs** up to the flight deck.
- P1: on the shelf over the upper-deck lounge. P3: on top of the crate stack in the hold.
- **Secret:** at the front of the hold, **paw prints** lead into the wall: it's a false wall. Behind it, a hatch to the **pet hold** (travelling pets asleep in their crates): P2 and a hidden 1UP.

**25-3 Wing Walk** (outside, in the sky)
- Out of the over-wing exit onto the wing, in bright daylight above the clouds. Gaps in the wing are a long way down. The **flaps tilt**: land on the high end and the flap flings you up. Behind each **engine** there's a gap in the wing full of **hot exhaust**: drop in on purpose and it carries you up (it never drops you). Where a wing panel is missing, a friendly **escort gull** in a blue scarf ferries you across. Piwi's gulls (red bandanas) swoop at you. A raised spoiler panel near the end needs a flap's fling to get over. The finish is by the winglet with its green light.
- P1: at the top of the first engine's hot air. P3: over the missing panel (jump from the escort gull).
- **Backstage (the Under-Stage) and a Wormhole → JAPAN (11-4):** one gap in the wing looks different: the **landing-gear doors hang open** under it and the **yellow escape slide is puffed up** down there (SOFT LANDING). Drop in. Brady lands on the slide in the landing-gear bay: P2, a hidden 1UP, a Wormhole back to Japan's Neon Tower, and a hatch back up onto the wing.

**25-4 Captain Gull** (the boss, in the cargo hold)
- A short walk through the hold past runaway carry-on bags (three postcards on the way), then the gull's corner of the hold, among crated animals and an empty PRETZELS container.
- Captain Gull, a stowaway seagull in a captain's cap, stole every pretzel on board. He keeps up in the rafters, out of reach, his **sack of pretzels dangling under him on a string**. **Stomp the sack**: the string yanks him down (YOINK!) and he lands dizzy. **Then stomp him.** Stomping him in the air just bounces off.
- He circles (the sack hangs low enough to reach from the crate stacks or the middle shelf), **swoops** low across the hold (the sack trails behind him near the floor: hop on it once he has passed), flings **pretzels that boomerang back**, calls **two of his crew** (after two hits) and **flaps a gale** across the hold (after four). Five hits; then he flies off without his pretzels.
- Beating him completes The Red-Eye.
