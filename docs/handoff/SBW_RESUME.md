## Powers carry between levels (`sbw/carry-powers`, 17 Nov)
Brady's request: a level cleared with powers starts the next level with them. `src/28_tour_dk_carry.js` (one hook line at the end of the Play constructor in `12_play.js`). On every clear (main, secret, boss) the loadout goes to `Save.data.tour.carry` (additive, same save key; old saves load fine): form (big / joint), the HANDS item (gear with its uses, or a signature item with its time left), the jump power (full gas), buff timers, and the time left on the star, the LSD flight and the trip colours, plus the picker's choice. The Backpack and the kandi bracelet keep their old rules. A death respawns Brady with nothing as before and leaves the store alone; quitting or GAME OVER leave it as it was on entry; a clear always overwrites it. A level that starts on a ride / autoscroll or underwater keeps the LSD flight and the flying carpet waiting for the next level that starts on foot. The clear banner now says YOU DID IT!. Skip audit (report only, in the test): a free flight gets past 17 key / switch requirements in 13 levels, a jump power (approximated) past 3 (6-2 corkscrew, 10-6 k1 / k3, all three levels already hold a jump power); no level becomes unfinishable.

## The hit ladder (`sbw/carry-powers`, 17 Nov)
Brady: a hit sometimes cost nothing (the Ecuador tortoise). Causes found: a power lost to a hit floated where Brady stood and he took it straight back, often in the same frame, so the hit cost nothing (any hit with gear, a signature item or a jump power, everywhere); El Tortugón's head reached 16 px past his hitbox. Now `src/28_tour_hc_hits.js`, the outermost tour wrapper of `Player.prototype.hurt`, takes ONE thing per hit: HANDS item (gear incl. Hard Hat, or signature item) -> jump power -> kandi -> joint -> big -> a life, with a 2 s flicker after each; the lost thing pops off and falls away (never collectable). Star blocks; Backpack, trips and buffs are untouched; pits / lava / crushers / timer still kill outright. The tortoise's head hurts like his body (`29_tour_ec.js`). Deliberate safe touches (reeling bosses, open weak spots, sleeping / hidden / underground / stunned enemies, thieves) are listed in `tools/sbw_hits_test.js`; a new unlisted one fails it. Pupo (`sbw/pupo`) wraps `Player.prototype.hurt` from a later file, so its star -> shield -> Pupo order runs before this ladder.
