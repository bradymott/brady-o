## The job (your land's six levels only, your own files only)
1. **Every secret room in your land is its own thing.** Five or six rooms, no two alike, each built from your land's theme, each with something to DO, not just a prize on a floor: a small puzzle or mini-game with a clear rule the player can read in two seconds and solve in 10-40 seconds (e.g. a memory/cup game, a switch-order puzzle, a weight or mirror puzzle, a timed coin dash, a rhythm/echo game, a tiny maze, a balance or aiming toy, a "spot the odd one out"; invent ones that suit your land: these are only examples of the scale). Reward scales with the challenge (premium item, 1UP, postcard-free: postcards stay in the level proper). Failing costs nothing but the prize or a retry. At least two rooms use a room kind/mini-game that exists nowhere else in the game; reuse of an existing typed kind is fine for others if re-skinned to your land.
2. **Not always out the way you came in.** At least half of the rooms have a different exit from their entrance: a chute that drops Brady further along the level, a second door that opens onto a ledge with a shortcut or a bonus, a lift into a rafters run. Every room always has a way out that cannot be missed, and no exit may skip a checkpoint's worth of level without the player choosing it.
3. **Hide them properly, fairly.** No more than one or two rooms in the land may be behind a plain door in plain sight. For the others the entrance is found by curiosity: a gap in a ceiling you can jump up through into a run ABOVE the level (a real stretch of 20-60 tiles with coins and a hazard or two, ending at the room or dropping back into the level), a floor you can drop through, a wall you can walk into, a place only your land's own mechanic can reach, an off-screen ledge hinted by a coin trail. Each has ONE subtle tell that a careful player can learn to read (a tile of slightly different shade, a draught of dust, a critter that looks up, coins that lead off-screen, a sound), consistent within your land. No tell = unfair; a door with a sign = too easy.
4. Keep the existing hidden exit to your x-6 level at least as well hidden as these (it gates a whole level: it should be the best secret in the land, still with a fair tell and the level's hint text).
5. The checker must reach every room, the run above, every exit and prize honestly (tell it only what the player can really do; play each entrance and each mini-game in your shots tool with real inputs). Pace and power-up budget still pass. Do not move postcards or checkpoints unless needed; say if you did.
