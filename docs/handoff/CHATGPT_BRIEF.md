# Brief for ChatGPT: ideas and concept art for Super Brady-O World

*From Brady, via Claude (who builds and tests the game). Paste this whole document into ChatGPT.*

## What this is

Super Brady-O World is my own 2D side-scrolling platformer that runs in a phone browser. Claude writes all the code and draws all the art in code. I would like your help with **ideas, writing and concept pictures**, which I will carry back to Claude to build.

You cannot see the game's code and you cannot test anything. So please **do not write code**. Your output is a single reply document (format at the end) that I paste back to Claude.

## The game in one minute

- **Hero:** Brady-O. Black tee and trousers, man bun, dark square sunglasses, blackwork tattoo sleeves.
- **His wife:** Chinka-O, a prima ballerina in a pink tutu, warm, funny, permanently unimpressed. She sends him postcards (three hidden in every level).
- **His steed:** Pupo, a giant, round, very slow-talking sloth ("...more... trip.").
- **Villain:** Piwi Squirmin', a runaway ventriloquist dummy and giggly show-off, who whisked Chinka-O off on his World Tour and shrank each country's landmark for his stage set.
- **Tone:** silly, warm, PG-13, a touch psychedelic. Jokes come from the place and the characters.
- **Look:** flat, bright "toon" shapes with a dark ink outline, readable on a phone.
- **Controls:** a stick, a JUMP button and a RUN/THROW button. Nothing else.

**Lands that exist (132 levels):** Mexico, Ecuador, Brazil, Mozambique, Morocco, Spain, France, Amsterdam, Bali, Vietnam, Japan, Nepal, then the "Encore Tour": Italy, Kenya, Australia, Iceland, the Moon. Hidden lands: Antarctica, World 22 (a glitchy rule-book world), The Red-Eye (a night flight), Festival Fields, Lost & Found (an airport's lost luggage).

**Level types that already exist (please do not repeat these):** auto-scroll chase, mine-cart rail, swimming and diving, climbing shafts, rising lava or water, digging, mazes, sneaking past guards, carrying an object, a thief chase, gliding, swinging, gong and beat levels on a rhythm, switch puzzles, a haunted house, gravity flips, a lamp in the dark, tide levels, parades, towers, rolling balls, a scooter dash, bouncy dough, a tipping tower, riding sound columns, a speed-boost dash, riding a stampede, steering giraffe necks, flamingo stepping stones, heat and shade, a burrow warren, kangaroo-pouch hopping, a road train in a headwind, sonar in the dark, spider-web trampolines, giant rolling fruit, northern-lights ribbons, tipping icebergs, sliding ground, sinking organ-pipe columns, "it only exists when you look away", low gravity, a jetpack-style spacewalk, a moon buggy, magnet boots on the ceiling, connect-the-stars.

## What I would like from you

### A. Twelve hidden lands (the big one)
We are growing the game from 132 to 200 levels with **hidden lands that only open when the player finds a really well-hidden secret**. Pitch 12 lands. They do not have to be countries: places, or stranger things (inside a snow globe, the back of a postcard, Piwi's prop cupboard, a karaoke bar at closing time) are welcome. For each land:
- Name, one-line pitch, the look in three or four colours and shapes.
- **Five or six levels**, each a *different kind of play the player controls with only a stick and two buttons*, not on the "already exists" list. One sentence on how it plays, one on what makes it hard near the end.
- A boss with a mechanic that comes from the land, three phases, and a clear warning before each attack.
- Four critters (enemies), each with one funny behaviour.
- Where and how its entrance might be hidden in an existing land (a ceiling you can jump through, a wall that is not there, something only a certain power-up reaches), and the single subtle clue that makes it fair.
- I would especially like several levels built around **climbing**: scrambling up, down and around on fences, nets, trellises, rigging or scaffolding with enemies sharing the surface (crossing to the back side, knocking enemies off from behind, and so on, in your own inventions).

### B. Sixty secret-room mini-games
Every level has a hidden room. I want each one different. Give me 60 tiny puzzle or mini-game ideas, each playable in 10 to 40 seconds with a stick and two buttons, with a rule the player understands in two seconds. (I liked a three-cups shuffle game and small puzzles.) One or two lines each. Group them: memory, timing, logic, aiming, balance, rhythm, observation, "trick" rooms. Say which existing land each would suit.

### C. A prize box for every land
In Vietnam the prize boxes are little bamboo cages with a round window, and I love that they belong to the place. Design a prize box for **each of the 22 lands listed above**: what it is made of, how it looks closed, what it does when Brady hits it from below, and what the "empty" version looks like. Then make **one concept picture per land** (or a few sheets of several): flat colours, bold dark outline, front view, roughly square, on a plain background, with no lettering in the picture.

### D. Chinka-O's writing
- A short "care package" note from Chinka-O for each of the 22 lands (two or three sentences, in her voice: dry, loving, a joke about the place), sent when Brady finds every postcard in that land.
- Thirty one-line postcard captions in her voice.

### E. Anything you think is missing
Up to ten short ideas for making the game more fun, or its secrets better hidden. Say why each is fun.

## What not to do
- **No code**, and no advice about engines or libraries.
- **Nothing that copies Nintendo or any other game, film, show or brand.** No Mario-style characters, enemies, power-ups, blocks, pipes, flags, level names or music. No brand names or logos. Fresh ideas only; "in the spirit of classic platformers" is as close as it gets.
- **Never caricature people.** No jokes at the expense of a nationality, religion or culture. No national or religious headwear on characters. Sacred buildings may only be far-off plain silhouettes. Humour about food, weather, animals, tourists and Piwi's ego is all fine.
- Keep it PG-13: no gore, no sex, no slurs.
- Do not rename or redesign Brady-O, Chinka-O, Pupo or Piwi Squirmin'.
- Do not invent controls beyond a stick and two buttons.
- Do not pad. A sharp idea in one line beats a paragraph.
