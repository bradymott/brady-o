# Relay to ChatGPT: a new app icon for Super Brady-O World

*Brady pastes this into ChatGPT, with the current icon attached (`dist/icons/world/icon-512.png`).*

## What I need
One new home-screen icon for my game, **Super Brady-O World**. Please make three different concepts first so I can pick one, then finish the one I choose.

## The game in two lines
A hand-made 2D platformer for iPhone: 200 levels across 34 lands. Brady-O tours the world on his sloth Pupo, putting back the landmarks that the villain Piwi Squirmin' (a ventriloquist dummy) shrank and stole, with postcards from his wife Chinka-O along the way. It is silly, warm and colourful, with hidden lands behind well-kept secrets.

## The current icon (attached)
Brady's grinning face in front of a blue-and-green globe, on an orange and yellow sunburst, with a little pink and cream plane. It is fine but flat and plain. I want something with more depth and more personality that still reads at a glance.

## Who must be on it
**Brady-O**, drawn as a friendly cartoon, not a realistic portrait:
- light-brown hair pulled back into a **man bun** on top of his head
- **dark square sunglasses**
- clean-shaven, **no stubble**, big open grin
- **black T-shirt**
- if his arms show: black geometric line tattoos on both arms

Optional, if it still reads small: **Pupo**, a round, soft, sleepy sloth (never spiky), peeking over his shoulder.

Leave out Chinka-O and Piwi: too many faces for an icon.

## The look
- Flat, bold cartoon style with a **thick dark outline** (the game calls it "Fiesta Toon"), bright fiesta colours: warm orange, sunny yellow, hot pink, turquoise, purple.
- More depth than now: soft shading, a highlight on the sunglasses, a rounded globe, a little shadow under the chin. Not glossy 3D, not photo-real.
- Ideas you may use or ignore: the globe behind him, a sunburst, a postcard, a tiny plane, a hint of a secret (a keyhole, a door ajar) since the game is full of hidden lands.

## Hard rules
- **No text or letters** anywhere on the icon.
- Nothing that resembles another company's game or character (no red caps, moustaches, mushrooms with spots, question-mark blocks, pipes, stars with eyes).
- No real landmarks, flags or religious buildings.
- It must read at **60 pixels wide**: one big face, one clear shape behind it, no small details that turn to mush.

## Technical
- A **square PNG, 1024 x 1024**, no transparency, no rounded corners (the phone rounds them itself), no border or frame.
- Keep everything important inside the **middle 80 %** of the square: some phones crop the edges into a circle, so the background colour must run right to every edge.
- Please also give me a version of the chosen icon with a **plain flat background** in case the busy one is too much at small size.

## What to send back
1. Three concepts, side by side, each also shown tiny (about 60 pixels) next to it.
2. After I pick: the final 1024 x 1024 PNG, plus the plain-background version.
